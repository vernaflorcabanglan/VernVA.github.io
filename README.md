<section id="why" class="section container">
  <h2>Why integrate AI as a VA?</h2>
  <p>AI can help you reduce repetitive work, increase output quality, and create new service lines (content, social, data prep, automations). Integration is about combining reliable tools with your expertise and client relationships.</p>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="card">
      <h4>Save time</h4>
      <p>Generate drafts, summarize long docs, and automate routine responses.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="card">
      <h4>Scale services</h4>
      <p>Deliver more for each client using templates, automations, and AI-assisted workflows.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="card">
      <h4>Differentiate</h4>
      <p>Offer specialized AI-enabled packages like "weekly content + analytics" or "AI email + follow-ups."</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="skills" class="section alt container">
  <h2>Core skills to build</h2>
  <ul class="skill-list">
    <li><strong>Communication:</strong> Clear client intake and expectations.</li>
    <li><strong>Prompting:</strong> Write reliable prompts and templates for consistent results.</li>
    <li><strong>Automation basics:</strong> Zapier/Make/IFTTT workflows, webhooks, and scheduling.</li>
    <li><strong>Privacy & security:</strong> Handling sensitive client data responsibly.</li>
    <li><strong>Tool literacy:</strong> Familiarity with major AI tools and integrations.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section id="roadmap" class="section container">
  <h2>6-step roadmap to launch</h2>
  <ol class="roadmap">
    <li><strong>Choose niche & services:</strong> e.g., social/media management, admin, customer support.</li>
    <li><strong>Master 2 AI tools:</strong> e.g., Chat-based model (ChatGPT), and an automation platform (Zapier/Make).</li>
    <li><strong>Create templates:</strong> Email responses, social captions, research prompts.</li>
    <li><strong>Build a portfolio:</strong> Before/after examples showing AI-assisted results.</li>
    <li><strong>Set pricing & packages:</strong> Offer a standard and an AI-powered premium tier.</li>
    <li><strong>Automate onboarding:</strong> Intake forms, scheduling, and initial data collection with automations.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
